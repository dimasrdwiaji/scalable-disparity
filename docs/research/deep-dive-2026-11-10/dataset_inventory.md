## 1. Executive Summary of Audit Outcomes

1. **Vector Footprint & Morphology Candidates**: *Google Open Buildings v3* (`CC-BY-4.0`) provides the richest Global South informal roof detection recall (including Indonesia), whereas *Overture Maps Buildings* (`ODbL`) merges OSM community edits with Microsoft/Google detections and *OpenStreetMap (OSM)* (`ODbL`) exhibits major completeness inequality across poorer urban clusters ([Herfort et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-39698-6); [Chamberlain et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compenvurbsys.2024.102104)). **`[Plausible]`** Vector orientation entropy ($H_{\text{orient}}$), micro-footprint shares ($< 30\text{ m}^2$, $< 50\text{ m}^2$), and within-cell Gini offer distinct candidate probes whose polygon-merge sensitivity must be screened side-by-side across footprint providers in Workstream C.
2. **Dasymetric & Gridded Deprivation Leakage Proof**:
   * **`[Established]` `GHS-POP R2023A`**: Disaggregates census counts (`GPWv4.11` adjusted to UN WPP) using **residential built volume** (`GHS-BUILT-V` minus `GHS-BUILT-V_NRES`), directly explaining why $\text{Volume}/\text{GHS-POP}$ collapsed in Jabodetabek ($R^2 = 0.976$ with built volume).
   * **`[Established]` `WorldPop (100m Constrained/Unconstrained)`**: Random Forest weights explicitly ingest **VIIRS nighttime lights**, OSM roads, land cover, and built-settlement extents -> **contains both NTL and built-up leakage**.
   * **`[Established]` `GRDI v1`**: Built from 6 components (SHDI, IMR, Child Dependency Ratio, Built-up Extent, VIIRS VNL, VNL Slope) at $\approx 1\text{ km}$ -> **unusable as an internal feature or local ground truth inside cities**.
   * **`[Established]` `Meta / CIESIN HRSL`**: Uses binary building presence detected on Maxar optical imagery without nighttime lights or building volume, proportionally allocating census tracts across built pixels -> **cleaner of NTL/height circularity than WorldPop/GHS-POP, though strictly bounded by coarse admin census resolution and baseline era (~2015–2021 imagery)**.
3. **Locked Exclusions Verified by Primary Specs**:
   * *Air Quality (`ACAG PM2.5`, `TROPOMI NO2`)* rejected ($1\text{ km}$ and $5.5 \times 3.5\text{ km}$ native grids cannot resolve H3 Res 8 disparity).
   * *OSM Sidewalks (`sidewalk=no`)* and *Building Setbacks* rejected (completeness bias and Euro/US *ruko* incompatibility).
   * *Mapillary* rejected as a primary indicator due to vehicular capture exclusion in narrow kampung *gangs*.
   * *Flood & Terrain Layers (`FABDEM`, `MERIT-Hydro HAND`, `InSAR`)* excluded from the universal global core and documented strictly as an **Optional Regional Hazard Module**.
4. **Locked Operational Signals to Screen in Workstream C**:
   * **Thermal Injustice**: *Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 `ST_B10`* (`100 m` native / `30 m` grid) evaluated as **intra-urban thermal disparity ($\Delta\text{LST}_{\text{intra}}$)** within the same urban temperature envelope (`GHS-SMOD` urban mask / local multi-km urban peer baseline) — **no regional elevation lapse-rate adjustment**.
   * **Overture Places Schema Migration Warning**: Primary Overture docs confirm schema **v2.0.0** (released Sept 2026) **removed legacy `categories`** in favor of `taxonomy` / `basic_category`, and added **Foursquare OS Places** (`2025-09-24`, `Apache 2.0`) alongside Meta (`CDLA-Permissive-2.0`). Accessibility must split **`A_public`** vs. **`A_commercial`** across Overture Places & OSM.

## 4. Operational Implications Going Into Workstream C (Pilot Screens)

Based on primary dataset mechanics, Workstream C (`pilot_screens.md`) must run three focused empirical diagnostics **before** any pipeline code changes:
1. **Morphology Source & Density Stratification Check**: Test $S_{<30\text{m}^2}$, $S_{<50\text{m}^2}$, Footprint Area Gini, and vector $H_{\text{orient}}$ from **Google Open Buildings v3** across confidence thresholds (`0.65`, `0.70`, `0.75`) vs. **Overture Buildings** inside Kotaku polygons, stratified strictly by **building density terciles** (proving separation is not merely a city-edge density proxy).
2. **Physical EO & Intra-Urban Thermal Check ($\Delta\text{LST}_{\text{intra}}$, NDVI, Texture)**:
   * Compute Landsat 8/9 dry-season cloud-masked median `ST_B10` inside `GHS-SMOD` urban settlement mask, testing **local intra-urban baseline anomaly ($\Delta\text{LST}_{\text{intra}}$)** within $3\text{ km}$ / $5\text{ km}$ / $10\text{ km}$ urban peer rings (without any lapse-rate equation) vs. Sentinel-2 dry-season `NDVI` / `NDBI` (and test vegetation-masked `10 m` Sentinel-2 GLCM vs. optional Sentinel-1 `VH/VV` if cached/applicable, checking valid-pair `NaN` dropout vs. density).
3. **Dual-Channel Accessibility Check (`A_public` vs. `A_commercial`)**:
   * Inspect Overture Places **Schema v2 taxonomy** (`taxonomy.primary`, `basic_category`) and OSM tags in Karawang & DKI Jakarta (after auditing `dki_rw_kumuh_pergub90.csv` geometry structure) to verify whether isolating formal **`A_public`** (schools, clinics/Puskesmas, transit) eliminates or reverses the `AUC = 0.30` sign-flip observed on collapsed POIs.
